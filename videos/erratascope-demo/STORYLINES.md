# Direction

Selected: **Follow the evidence** — one actual review, starting at the workspace, tracing claims back to the manufacturer, preserving unknowns, then recording and exporting a human decision. Real UI is the visual evidence. Silence and short explanatory captions follow the user's brief.

Alternatives considered: **Export first**, opening on the review-summary artifact and tracing its origin backwards; **Unknowns first**, opening on the synthetic omission case then showing why manufacturer evidence matters. The forward workflow makes a first-time judge's task easiest to follow, so the selected direction follows the supplied demo outline.
